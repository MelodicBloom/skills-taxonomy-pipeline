# MelodicBloom Decision Review Engine

This repository is the observational reviewer for MelodicBloom site, product, design-system, and agent-runtime work. Its purpose is not to produce more ideas. Its purpose is to make ideas legible enough to challenge.

The engine receives evidence from observational repositories, tokenizes what is materially present, separates observation from inference, compares plausible strategies, and produces a review package that explains the decision architecture. A review must show what changed, why the change is being considered, which alternatives were viable, what each alternative preserves or sacrifices, where uncertainty remains, and what evidence would reverse the recommendation.

The reviewer does not treat style as decoration or taste as a mysterious personal preference. Style is represented as a coordinated system of hierarchy, rhythm, density, material, typography, color, motion, interaction, narrative stance, cultural reference, and restraint. Taste is evaluated through coherence, specificity, proportion, contextual fit, disciplined surprise, legibility, craft, and the quality of what the proposal refuses to add.

The engine may defend an idea, reject it, recommend a narrower version, or declare that the available evidence is insufficient. It may not approve its own implementation, deploy changes, mutate customer data, or convert aesthetic preference into a false factual claim.

Every review ends with a bottom line written for a human decision-maker. That bottom line states the recommended move, confidence, principal opportunity, principal risk, cost of delay, cost of error, reversibility, and the next piece of evidence worth buying.

The current repository name is a temporary substrate. The intended repository identity is `MelodicBloom/decision-review-engine` or `MelodicBloom/observatory-reviewer`.
